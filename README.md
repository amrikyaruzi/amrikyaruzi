## Hi, I'm Amri 👋

I'm a physician (MD, MPH) working in **healthcare quality and patient safety** in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Most of my day is turning hospital data into decisions: indicators, control charts, surveys and reports.

I'm moving towards **health data science**: analysis, reproducible reporting and teaching. In my spare time I run a self-hosted home lab to learn Linux, networking and infrastructure properly.

### What I work on

- 📈 **Quality improvement analytics:** statistical process control (SPC), indicator dashboards and automated reports in R
- 🧪 **Clinical and public-health research:** survey-weighted regression, survival analysis and logistic models, including analysis support for postgraduate dissertations
- 🧑‍🏫 **Teaching** data analytics to health professionals
- 🖥️ **Self-hosting:** Proxmox, TrueNAS, Docker and Home Assistant
